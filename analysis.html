How the status is calculated

Provisional thresholds
  1. LOWBelow 30 µJ
  2. NOTICE30 to <100 µJ
  3. CAUTION100 to <300 µJ
  4. WARNING300 to <500 µJ
  5. CRITICAL500 µJ or above

Low retained energy returns the indicator to green. High-energy bursts can still raise the status even when there are few events. Activity and trend provide context; status follows the energy thresholds above.

These thresholds have not been validated against landslide outcomes. Green means low measured acoustic energy; it does not establish slope stability. Landslide prediction accuracy is unmeasured until labeled site data is available.

Only live WARNING and CRITICAL states trigger enabled alerts.
